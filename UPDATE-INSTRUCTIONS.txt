DUEL CLUB — FULL IMAGE VOTING UPDATE

What changed:
- The two big contender cards remain side-by-side at roughly 50% width each.
- Photos, GIFs, and videos fit entirely inside their card instead of being cropped.
- Depending on aspect ratio, empty space will appear around the media. This is intentional.
- All pack features, voting logic, and video controls remain unchanged.

HOW TO UPDATE YOUR RENDER SITE
1. Unzip this archive.
2. In https://github.com/lowerx/Duel-Club open the public folder.
3. Replace public/styles.css with the one from this archive.
4. Commit the change to the main branch.
5. Allow Render to redeploy, then hard-refresh your site (Cmd + Shift + R).

Only the CSS file changes. Don't replace or remove any other file.
