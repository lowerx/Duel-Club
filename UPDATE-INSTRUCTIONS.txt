DUEL CLUB — FULL-SCREEN VOTING UPDATE
=====================================

This update is based on the latest Delete Packs version and retains:
- 256 contenders per pack
- GIF and video support
- Collaborative studios
- Pack deletion and creator ownership checks

WHAT CHANGED
- In a live match, both choices sit side-by-side and each takes almost
  half the browser window width.
- Photos and GIFs become much taller.
- Match statistics move below the matchup so they no longer squeeze the cards.
- Mobile screens also show two large choices side-by-side.
- You can now click/tap photos or GIFs themselves to vote.
  Videos remain playable without accidentally voting; use the vote button.

UPDATE YOUR EXISTING GITHUB/RENDER SITE
1. Unzip this archive.
2. Open your GitHub repository's public/ folder.
3. Upload/replace the two files from this ZIP's public/ folder:
      app.js
      styles.css
4. Commit the changes to main.
5. Wait for Render to automatically deploy.
6. On your site, hard refresh (Cmd + Shift + R).

DO NOT replace or delete index.html, server.js, package.json,
pack data, or any other existing file.

TESTED
- 20 automated tests passed, including creator-only deletion and 256-entry packs.
- Layout checked at 1440px, 768px and 390px widths with a browser preview;
  in all cases cards were equal width with no horizontal scrolling.
- Not yet tested against your live Render deployment.
